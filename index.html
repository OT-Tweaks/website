Questions

Something else? Ask in our Discord.

How much will my FPS go up?

It depends on your PC. The biggest gains come on PCs still running stock Windows settings — power-saving plans, background recording, lots of background apps. You'll usually notice steadier frame times and fewer stutters before a higher average FPS. We don't promise a number, because no honest optimizer can.

Is it safe for my PC?

Yes. OT Tweaks only changes Windows settings, makes a restore point before Full Optimize, and lets you undo any single tweak or roll everything back to Windows defaults. It never disables your antivirus, firewall or security updates.

Can I get banned in games?

OT Tweaks changes Windows settings only. It never reads or modifies game files or game memory, and never touches game or anti-cheat processes. Game profiles also skip tweaks that kernel-level anti-cheats are sensitive to. OT Tweaks is not affiliated with any game publisher.

Will it work on my PC?

OT Tweaks runs on 64-bit Windows 10 and 11, on desktops and laptops, with NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics. It detects your hardware and applies the right settings for it.

How do I get the app after I pay?

Right after checkout you'll see a page with the download. Then open a ticket in our Discord with your order email and we'll send your login, usually within a few hours.

Can I use it on more than one PC?

Each license works on one PC — it locks to the first PC you sign in on. Got a new PC? Click "Request a PC change" in the app; once our team approves it, the app unlocks on the new PC automatically.

Do I have to run it every time I play?

No. The tweaks are permanent, and Always-On keeps them applied after reboots and Windows updates. Game Boost is optional if you want to free extra RAM before a session.

How do updates work?

Automatically. The app downloads new versions in the background and shows a "Restart to update" button. Updates are free for the life of your license.

What's your refund policy?

See our refund policy. If OT Tweaks doesn't work on your PC and we can't fix it, you get your money back.
