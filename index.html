Questions

How much will my FPS go up?

It depends on your PC. The biggest gains come on PCs still running stock Windows settings, with a power-saving plan, background recording and lots of background apps. You'll usually notice steadier frame times and fewer stutters before you notice a higher average FPS. We don't promise a number, because no honest optimizer can.

Is it safe for my PC?

Yes. OT Tweaks only changes Windows settings, makes a restore point before Full Optimize, and has one-click rollback to Windows defaults. It never disables your antivirus, firewall or Windows security updates.

Can I get banned in games?

OT Tweaks changes Windows settings only. It never reads or modifies game files or game memory. Game profiles also skip tweaks that kernel-level anti-cheats are sensitive to. OT Tweaks is not affiliated with any game publisher.

Will it work on my PC?

OT Tweaks runs on 64-bit Windows 10 and 11, on desktops and laptops, with NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics. It detects your hardware and applies the right settings for it.

How do I get the app after I pay?

Can I use it on more than one PC?

Each license works on one PC. It's linked to the first PC you sign in on. If you get a new PC, message support and we'll move your license.

Do I have to run it every time I play?

No. The tweaks are permanent, and Always-On keeps them applied after reboots and Windows updates. Game Boost is optional if you want to free extra RAM before a session.
