What usually holds Apex Legends back

CPU-bound fights

Big team fights push the CPU. Windows scheduling and power settings decide how well it keeps up.

MPO stutter

Multiplane overlay can cause stutter and flicker on some GPU and monitor setups.

Game DVR overhead

Background recording hooks cost frames.

Background apps

Launchers and overlays waking up mid-fight.

What the Apex Legends profile applies

Open Game Profiles in OT Tweaks, pick Apex Legends, and it applies:

  • Zero Input Delay
  • HAGS
  • Disable MPO
  • Game Priority
  • CPU Scheduling
  • Mouse Polling
  • Disable Game DVR
  • Kill Background Apps
Easy Anti-Cheat. User-mode anti-cheat. Generally safe with most tweaks, HPET/MSI still skipped as a precaution.

Settings worth checking in Apex Legends

  • Turn on NVIDIA Reflex in Apex's video settings if you have an NVIDIA card.
  • Keep your GPU driver up to date. OT Tweaks' GPU driver check tells you if it's old.
  • Cap FPS just under what your PC holds in fights.

Easy to undo

Every tweak has its own Undo button, and Rollback All puts Windows back to its defaults. Full Optimize makes a restore point before it changes anything. Results depend on your hardware, so we don't promise a specific FPS number.
