What usually holds Fortnite back

CPU spikes in build fights

Lots of structures at once leans on the CPU. Windows scheduling and priority settings matter here.

Shader compilation

First matches after an update or driver install stutter while shaders build.

Multiplane overlay (MPO) stutter

A known Windows display feature that causes flicker and stutter on some setups.

Mouse and polling settings

Inconsistent polling can make aim feel uneven at high FPS.

Settings worth checking in Fortnite

  • Try Performance mode in Fortnite's rendering settings on lower-end PCs.
  • Play a match or two after updates so shaders finish compiling.
  • If you get kicked for VPN or IP reasons, close ping boosters and VPNs first.

Easy to undo

Every tweak has its own Undo button, and Rollback All puts Windows back to its defaults. Full Optimize makes a restore point before it changes anything. Results depend on your hardware, so we don't promise a specific FPS number.
