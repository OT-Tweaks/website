What usually holds Valorant back

Background apps stealing frames

Launchers, overlays and updaters wake up mid-round and cause one-off hitches.

Windows timer and power settings

Default power plans park cores and throttle the CPU between frames.

Network buffering

Nagle's algorithm and network throttling can add delay to small packets.

Game DVR and overlays

Xbox Game Bar recording hooks add overhead even when you aren't recording.

Settings worth checking in Valorant

  • Use fullscreen, not windowed fullscreen.
  • Turn on NVIDIA Reflex in Valorant's video settings if you have an NVIDIA card.
  • Cap your FPS a little below what your PC can hold so frame times stay even.
