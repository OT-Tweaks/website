Getting started

How do I get the app after paying?

Your account is created automatically the moment your payment goes through. Check your inbox for an email from noreply@ot-tweaks.firebaseapp.com with a link to set your password (look in spam too).

  1. Download the installer from ottweaks.com/download.
  2. Sign in with the email you used at checkout and the password you just set.

No email after 15 minutes? Open a ticket in our Discord and we'll check your order.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

That's SmartScreen. It shows for newer apps that haven't built up download history yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

What should I do first?
  1. Click Full Optimize on the dashboard. It makes a Windows restore point before it changes anything.
  2. Turn on Always-On so your tweaks stay applied after every reboot.
  3. Restart your PC.
Which PCs does it work on?

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit, with NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics. OT Tweaks detects your GPU and applies the settings that fit it.

Account & license

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, click Forgot password? and we'll email you a reset link. You can also open a ticket in our Discord.

I got a new PC or reinstalled Windows

Your license works on one PC at a time. Open the app on the new PC, sign in, and click Request a PC change on the lock screen. Once the team approves it, the app unlocks by itself. No need to reinstall or sign in again.

Can I share my account?

No. A license locks to the first PC you sign in on, so a shared login won't work on a second PC.

Can I get a refund?

See our refund policy. In short: within 14 days if you haven't signed in yet, or if it doesn't work on your PC and support can't fix it. Open a ticket in Discord to ask.

Undo & safety

How do I undo a tweak?

Every active tweak has an Undo button on its card. To put everything back at once, go to Restore & Undo and click Rollback All, then restart.

Will it get me banned?

OT Tweaks only changes Windows settings. It never touches games, game files or game memory. Game Profiles go further: they match each game's anti-cheat and skip tweaks that kernel-level anti-cheats are sensitive to, like HPET and MSI Mode changes.

Does it turn off my antivirus or Windows features?

No. Your antivirus and firewall stay on, and Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, audio, Search, Windows Hello, Game Pass and Xbox sign-in are left alone.

Fixing problems

Games still stutter
  1. Run Full Optimize again and make sure Always-On is on.
  2. Restart your PC.
  3. In Tools & Repair, run the GPU driver check and try the MPO stutter fix.
My monitor is stuck at 60 Hz

Run Monitor Refresh Check in Tools & Repair. It shows your real refresh rate and how to switch to your monitor's full rate. You can also set it in Windows under Display settings → Advanced display.

Ping spikes when someone else uses the internet

That's usually bufferbloat. Run the Lag under load test in the Network tab and follow the router steps it gives you. Ethernet helps a lot over Wi-Fi.

Kicked from a game for VPN, IP or machine reasons

Close VPNs and ping boosters (ExitLag, WTFast, NoPing, Cloudflare WARP), turn off proxies, don't use Wi-Fi and Ethernet at the same time, and restart your router. 5G home internet can trigger this because many people share one IP. If it keeps happening, contact the game's support.

The app says it needs Administrator

Right-click OT Tweaks and choose Run as administrator. Windows settings can't be changed without it.

An update is stuck

Go to Settings → Updates and click Check now. If it still fails, reinstall from ottweaks.com/download. Your tweaks and settings are kept.
